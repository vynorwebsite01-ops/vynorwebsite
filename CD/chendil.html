Who We Are

VYNOR PRIVATE LIMITED is a technology startup focused on building AI-driven systems across healthcare and safety domains. The company operates as a parent organization leading multiple innovative projects, each targeting critical gaps in real-world systems.

With a strong foundation in system design, embedded technology, and application development, VYNOR builds solutions that are scalable, efficient, and deployment-ready. Our goal is to create a unified ecosystem where intelligent systems work together to improve lives.

VYNOR is the parent company behind three active product lines: AUSWAY, AUSCAM, and A.V. Monitoring — each targeting a critical gap in real-world emergency and safety infrastructure.
